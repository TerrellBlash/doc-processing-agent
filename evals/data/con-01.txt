MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of February 27, 2026 (the
"Effective Date") by and between Vantage Point Consulting, a Georgia company ("Provider"),
and Quartzline Printing, a Delaware company ("Client").

1. SCOPE. Provider shall perform the services described in each Statement of Work signed
by both parties, with the skill and care of a qualified professional.

2. TERM. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated under
Section 6.

3. PAYMENT. Client shall pay Provider the fees set out in each Statement of Work. Provider
will invoice monthly, and Client shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of
receipt.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information
secret, use it only to perform this Agreement, and return or destroy it when this
Agreement ends.

5. WARRANTIES. Each party warrants that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement
and that performing it will not breach any other agreement it is bound by.

6. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 30 days'
written notice, or immediately if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and
does not cure the breach within 15 days of written notice.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.
Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the twelve months before the
claim.

8. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, without
regard to its conflict of laws rules.

9. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement is the parties' entire agreement on its subject and
replaces all earlier proposals and understandings.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
