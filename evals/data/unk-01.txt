LEMON BARS
Serves 16

Ingredients
- 1 cup butter, softened
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- 2 cups flour
- 4 eggs
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 1/3 cup lemon juice

Steps
1. Heat the oven to 350 F.
2. Mix butter, powdered sugar and flour; press into a 9x13 pan and bake 20 minutes.
3. Whisk eggs, sugar and lemon juice; pour over the crust.
4. Bake 25 minutes more, cool, and dust with powdered sugar.
